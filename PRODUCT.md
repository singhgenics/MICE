# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Corporate meeting planners: in-house event, travel, and marketing managers at large companies who choose destinations and vendors for conferences, incentive trips, board meetings, and product launches. They compare 3-6 destinations and DMCs before shortlisting, usually starting the search alone at night or between other work, then bring a shortlist to internal stakeholders. Secondary audience: PCOs and association organizers evaluating the destination for congresses and exhibitions, who care more about infrastructure scale and delegate logistics than lifestyle appeal.

## Product Purpose

Confluence is a MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) destination gateway: a single site that makes the case for booking large-scale corporate events at its partner destinations and venues, and converts a planner's interest into a submitted event brief. Success is a qualified proposal request: a planner who arrived comparing destinations leaves having submitted their event details and expecting a callback.

## Positioning

Confluence is the destination people actually want to travel to for a mandatory event — not just a venue with the right square footage, but the reason attendees stop grumbling about "another offsite."

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Request a Proposal (opens the event-brief form)
- Secondary CTA: Explore Destinations (for planners not ready to commit details yet)
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this is a place worth flying people to, that also happens to run a flawless conference.
- Belief ladder: (1) this destination is genuinely desirable, not just logistically convenient → (2) the infrastructure and delivery track record can handle an event at our scale → (3) other companies like ours have already run successful events here → (4) submitting a brief costs me five minutes and gets me a real, specific response.
- Proof on hand: none supplied yet. Case studies, delegate/attendee counts, and named client logos are represented with clearly fictional placeholder detail (invented company names, plausible but non-verifiable figures) until real proof is supplied. Flag this to the user before launch.

## Brand Personality

Polished and corporate-premium, with a travel magazine's warmth underneath: confident rather than cold, precise rather than stiff. Voice speaks like a five-star hospitality brand crossed with a global business publication — declarative, specific, never gushing. It never uses filler enthusiasm ("elevate," "seamless," "unlock"); it earns confidence through specificity (real numbers, named capacities, named rooms).

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS "enterprise gateway" template (mesh-gradient hero, sky-blue-and-orange palette, "solutions by role" mega-menu). Not a government tourism-board brochure (stock photo carousels, flag icons, generic "discover paradise" copy). Not cream/sand AI-editorial-by-default, not gradient text, not a numbered-eyebrow scaffold on every section.

## Design Principles

- Sell the destination first, the conference facility second — the emotional case for travel has to land before the logistics case.
- Every proof point is specific (a named room, a capacity number, a named client) — never a vague superlative.
- One signature visual motif (convergence: many origins, one destination) recurs across hero, transitions, and data visualization rather than decorating a single page.
- The proposal form is never more than one click away, but never interrupts the browsing experience with a modal or popup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor: 4.5:1 body text contrast, visible keyboard focus states, full keyboard operability of nav/menus/forms, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all scroll and hero motion, alt text on every meaningful image.
